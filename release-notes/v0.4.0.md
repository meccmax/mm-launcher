First public release of the **MM Launcher**, the Misfit Mercenaries DayZ launcher.

## Downloads
- **MMLauncher.exe**: the launcher for players. Just run it; nothing to install. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the exe isn't code-signed yet: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **MMServerAgent.zip**: for MM server admins only. See `AGENT-SETUP.txt` inside.
- **SHA256SUMS.txt**: checksums the launcher uses to verify updates.

## What it does
- Finds every MM server automatically and shows live status: players, queue, map, in-game time, ping, required DLC and uptime.
- Shows each server's full, verified mod list and what you have installed.
- **JOIN SERVER**: downloads missing or outdated mods through Steam, then starts DayZ with BattlEye straight into the server.
- **Update mods**, **Verify files** and **Repair** for broken or missing mods.
- **Report a problem**: sends your DayZ logs and crash info to staff in one click. Your Windows username is removed first.
- **Settings**: Steam, DayZ and mod folders are found automatically or can be set by hand, plus launch options.
- From this version on, the launcher tells you when a new version is out and updates itself in one click.
