## New: color themes
**Settings > Appearance** now has seven color schemes: MM Orange (default), Arctic Blue, Toxic Green, Blood Red, Royal Purple, Steel and **Daylight**, a light theme. Pick one to see it straight away; Save keeps it, Cancel goes back.

## New: anonymous usage stats (you choose)
The first time you open this version, the launcher asks whether to send anonymous usage stats to MM. It helps us see what works and what breaks: how long mod downloads take, which errors and crashes happen, and how the MM servers are used.
- **Nothing is sent unless you say yes.** You can change your answer any time in **Settings > Usage stats**.
- **What's sent:** launcher and Windows version, joins and play time on MM servers, how many times you join other servers (never which ones), mod download times, errors and crashes.
- **Anonymous:** no names, Steam IDs or IP addresses are kept. Your launcher is told apart by a random number that's deleted if you turn stats off.
- **Only to MM's own servers,** over the encrypted connection.

## Server admins
Run **update-agent.bat** as administrator. Stats from players who said yes are saved in the new `usage` folder next to the agent. Open `usage\summary.txt` for an overview of the last 7 and 30 days. Turn it off with `"AcceptUsageStats": false` in `agent.jsonc`.

## Downloads
- **MMLauncher-Setup.exe**: the launcher for players, installed with shortcuts. Updates itself in one click.
- **MMLauncher.exe**: the same launcher as a single portable file.
- **MMServerAgent.zip**: for MM server admins. See `AGENT-SETUP.txt` inside.
- **SHA256SUMS.txt**: checksums the launcher and agent use to verify updates.
