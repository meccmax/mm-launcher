Fixed: after a server restart for mod updates, JOIN SERVER and Update mods could say "Mods ready" while a mod was still on its old version. That happened when Steam hadn't noticed the new upload yet.

Now the launcher checks the Workshop at the moment you click JOIN, makes Steam fetch every mod that's out of date, and checks afterwards that each one really is the new version. If Steam still won't update a mod, the launcher doesn't start DayZ. Instead it tells you which mod it is and offers Repair (a fresh download, then it joins) or Join anyway.
